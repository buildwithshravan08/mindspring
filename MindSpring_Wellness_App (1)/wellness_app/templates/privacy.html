Privacy, Data Ethics & Safety

How {{ app_name }} protects student privacy, stores records, and safeguards wellbeing.

1. Data Storage & Encryption

Account passwords are encrypted using one-way cryptographic salts (Werkzeug PBKDF2). Survey responses and micro-activity session records are stored in a local SQLite database and are strictly utilized to compute your personal dashboard metrics. We never sell student data or serve third-party advertising.

2. Right to Complete Erasure

You retain full sovereignty over your digital footprint. You can permanently wipe your account, survey records, and activity logs at any time by visiting your Settings page and typing "DELETE".

3. Non-Clinical Scope & Safe Practice

{{ app_name }} is an academic Project-Based Learning tool created to test the efficacy of micro-breaks on student focus and stress. It is not an FDA/CDSCO certified medical device and should not be used to diagnose, treat, or mitigate psychiatric disorders.
